## 🧭 Using the Atlas

The main screen features a round Earth model. Use your mouse to navigate:

*   Rotate the Earth: Click and hold the left mouse button while moving the mouse.
*   Zoom: Scroll the mouse wheel up to zoom in or down to zoom out.
*   Move the Map: Click and hold the right mouse button to shift your view without rotating.

The toolbar at the bottom of the screen allows you to toggle different data layers. You can switch between active fire perimeters, historic incident reports, and wind overlays. Click on any fire marker to view specific details about the source, the date of the event, and the recorded size of the perimeter.

## 📊 Understanding the Data

EmberAtlas connects to satellite networks to provide current information. Because the application pulls data from sources like NASA and Copernicus, the map updates whenever you refresh the view. 

*   Heat Maps: Areas with higher intensity show in brighter colors, such as orange and red.
*   Wind Overlays: Moving lines indicate wind direction and speed, which helps explain fire spread patterns.
*   Exposure Data: This layer shows how close fire perimeters come to known structures or forest regions. This helps users visualize the risk level for various areas.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting Common Issues

If the application does not load data, check your internet connection first. The software requires a live connection to pull satellite imagery.

If the 3D model looks choppy or slow, adjust your settings:

1. Open the "Settings" menu inside the application.
2. Navigate to "Graphics."
3. Select "Low" or "Medium" quality. This reduces the demand on your computer hardware. 

If the application crashes, verify that your graphics drivers are up to date. Most users find success by visiting the website of their graphics card manufacturer and downloading the latest drivers for their specific hardware. 

You may also clear the application cache to fix temporary errors. To do this, close the application, delete the contents of the `AppData/Local/EmberAtlas/Cache` folder, and restart the software.
